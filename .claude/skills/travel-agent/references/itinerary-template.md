# Itinerary Template - Day-by-Day Structure

Every day in a travel-agent itinerary follows this shape. Pacing wins. Specifics over generics. One named anchor per day plus a named backup.

---

## Day Header

```
## Day N - [Date, Day of Week]
**Theme:** [one phrase - "Old Town Walk + Market", "Day of Art", "Coastal Drive"]
**Energy level:** [Low / Medium / High]
**Weather note:** [check current forecast - flag if rain probable, heat advisory, etc.]
```

---

## Block Structure

```
### Morning
- **Anchor:** [Named experience, with the reason it earns the slot]
- **Travel:** [walking time / transit, stated explicitly]
- **Eat:** [Named restaurant / café, with reason]
- **Backup if [weather / energy / closure]:** [Named alternative]

### Afternoon
- **Anchor:** ...
- **Travel:** ...
- **Eat:** ...
- **Backup:** ...

### Evening
- **Anchor:** ...
- **Travel:** ...
- **Eat:** [Named restaurant - RESERVATION REQUIRED if applicable, with how far ahead to book]
- **Backup:** ...
```

---

## Day-Type Variations

### Day 1 - Arrival / Jet-Lag Day

Light. Walkable. Near the hotel. **No anchor experiences requiring sharp focus.** This is non-negotiable.

```
### Morning (likely arrival)
- Land, transfer to hotel, leave bags if too early to check in
- Light walk - neighborhood orientation, not sightseeing

### Afternoon
- Easy lunch - sit-down, near hotel
- One landmark walk-by (no entry tickets)
- Hotel rest block - 90+ minutes

### Evening
- Early dinner - Named restaurant, reservation if needed, walking distance
- In bed by local bedtime - start the time-zone reset
```

### Travel / Transition Days

Move days are not sightseeing days. Plan one anchor at the morning OR the evening of a transit day, not both.

```
### Morning
- Pack out, breakfast, transfer to station/airport
- Travel block: [HH:MM hours]

### Afternoon
- Arrive, check in, light walk
- Snack/coffee - not a big meal

### Evening
- One easy anchor - named, walkable, no major effort
- Dinner near new hotel
```

### Departure Day

Plan the morning, leave the afternoon flexible, build in transit buffer (3 hours before international flights, 2 before domestic).

---

## Restaurant Format - The Reason Is Mandatory

Never:
- "Local restaurant in the area"
- "Try the food"
- "Find a good cafe"

Always:
- **[Restaurant Name]** - [why this one] - [reservation status]
  - "Trattoria da Enzo al 29 - the carbonara every Roman cab driver agrees on, and it's a 4-minute walk from the Day 2 hotel. Walk-in until 7:30 PM, reservation required after."
  - "Den - three Michelin stars, the most playful tasting menu in Tokyo, and the chef does a signature Monaka course that's worth the trip alone. Books 2 months ahead via Pocket Concierge."

---

## Anchor Experience Format

Every anchor needs:
1. **Named experience** - not "a museum," but "the Borghese Gallery"
2. **The reason it earns the slot** - why this over the alternatives
3. **Time block** - duration estimate
4. **Booking note** - required, optional, walk-in OK
5. **Lens fit** - one line tying it back to the traveler's stated passion

Example:
```
**Anchor: Borghese Gallery (10:00–12:00)**
- Why this one: Bernini sculptures in the rooms they were carved for -
  not a museum-as-warehouse experience. Strict 2-hour visit windows
  keep crowds tolerable.
- Booking: Required, books out 2–3 weeks ahead. Buy via the official
  Galleria Borghese site.
- Lens fit: For the art-passion traveler, this is the most concentrated
  hit of Bernini and Caravaggio in one room set in Italy.
```

---

## Backup Format

Every block needs a backup. Categories:
- **Weather backup** - if it rains
- **Energy backup** - if the traveler is wrecked
- **Closure backup** - if the anchor is unexpectedly closed

Example:
```
**Backup if rained out:** Pivot to the Capitoline Museums - covered,
20-minute walk, and the She-Wolf bronze plus Marcus Aurelius
equestrian are worth the swap.
**Backup if energy crashes:** Skip the afternoon block, nap at hotel,
move dinner earlier.
```

---

## Pacing Tells - Self-Check

Before finalizing a day, run this checklist:
- [ ] One signature anchor per major time block? (No more.)
- [ ] At least one rest block?
- [ ] Travel time stated between every stop?
- [ ] Every restaurant named with a reason?
- [ ] Every anchor has a backup?
- [ ] No anchor experiences on Day 1?
- [ ] Sunday/Monday don't fall on closure-heavy days for this city?
- [ ] One free afternoon per 3-day stretch for spontaneity?

If any answer is "no," fix the day before moving on.

---

## End-of-Trip "Vibe Map" (Optional, Earns Its Place)

At the end of the itinerary, a one-paragraph "what each day is *for*" map helps the traveler see the shape:

```
**The shape of the trip:**
- Days 1–2: Land soft, recover, and orient in the old town.
- Days 3–4: The food-passion days - market mornings, cooking class
  Day 3 evening, the signature multi-course Day 4.
- Day 5: Transit + light evening in [next city].
- Days 6–7: Art days - museums front-loaded, gallery walk Day 7.
- Day 8: Slow, free, departure.
```

This gives the traveler the *why* of the sequence - not just the what.
